# Chapter content

- `docs/CONTENT_AUTHORING.md` is the contract and `data/chapters/os-cpu-scheduling.json` the
  exemplar: match its depth, tone and field shapes. `scripts/check-manifest.mjs` is the schema.
- Validate one chapter with `node scripts/check-manifest.mjs --chapter <id>` before the full
  `npm run check`.
- `data/subjects.json` fixes the 45 chapter ids and the page ranges a chapter may cite. Never
  rename an id: every file under `data/chapters/` and `memory/chapters/` is named by it.
- Every item of `data/official-syllabus.json` that maps to the chapter needs a
  `syllabusCoverage` entry; `partial` and `curated` point at a curated topic.
- `data/curriculum_manifest.json` is generated by `scripts/build-manifest.mjs`. Never edit it.
- Topic ids (`<chapter>.<slug>`) are referenced from `memory/` derivations and PYQs and from the
  visuals registry. Renaming one breaks them; grep for it first.
