Turnaround & Waiting Time Core Metric
TAT = CT − AT  ·  WT = TAT − BT
CT: Completion Time  |  AT: Arrival Time  |  BT: Burst Time
Exponential Burst Averaging Prediction
τₙ₊₁ = α·tₙ + (1 − α)·τₙ
α: Smoothing factor [0,1]  |  tₙ: actual burst  |  τₙ: predicted burst
Total Disk Head Movement Disk Seek
D = Σ |cᵢ − cᵢ₋₁|
cᵢ: Cylinder track at step i  |  c₀: Initial cylinder head position
