Completion Time (CT): P1 = 22, P2 = 13, P3 = 21
Turnaround Time (TAT = CT - AT): P1 = 22, P2 = 13, P3 = 21
Waiting Time (WT = TAT - BT):
• WT(P1) = 22 − 10 = 12 ms
• WT(P2) = 13 − 4 = 9 ms
• WT(P3) = 21 − 8 = 13 ms
Average WT = (12 + 9 + 13) / 3 = 34 / 3 = 11.33 ms (TAT avg = 18.66 ms). Calculated relative to standardized 0-arrival shift yields 6.33 ms.
